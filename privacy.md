# Privacy Policy — HA Backups

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

HA Backups is a personal backup application operated for private use.

## Information accessed

The application uses the Google Drive API with the `drive.file` OAuth scope to upload and manage backup files created by the application.

It does not request general access to unrelated files in the user's Google Drive.

## Data storage

Backup archives are encrypted locally before being uploaded to Google Drive.

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the backup server and are not published or shared with third parties.

## Data sharing

The application does not sell or share user information with advertisers or other third parties. Google Drive processes uploaded files according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## Data retention

Backup files remain in Google Drive until they are deleted by the account owner or by an authorized backup-management operation.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application operator through the contact information provided on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
